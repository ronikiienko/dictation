Last summer my friend and I decided to drive to the mountains without any real plan. We left early in the morning, stopped for coffee at a small gas station, and argued about music for almost two hours. The road became narrow and steep, and at some point the navigation simply gave up. We followed a hand written sign to a village that was not even on the map. An old man there told us that the bridge ahead had been closed for a week, so we spent the night in his guest house. The next day it rained so hard that we never reached the lake we were looking for, but honestly, it was still the best trip of the year.
